# DVTM: Damn Vulnerable Threat Model

A hands-on threat modeling lab, like DVWA but for design.

**Open the lab:** https://vipxi.github.io/threat-modeling-lab/

## What you'll practise
- Marking trust boundaries on a data flow diagram
- Classifying threats with STRIDE and rejecting distractors
- Choosing mitigations and building a threat register
- Modeling your own system in the sandbox

## Labs
| Lab | Difficulty |
| --- | --- |
| ShopSmart online store | Beginner |
| PayFlow mobile banking | Intermediate |
| MediTrack patient portal | Advanced |

Pick a security level (Low, Medium or High) at the top of the page.
Progress is saved in your browser only.

All systems in these labs are fictional and for training only.
